# E04 · The `:settings` menu

**You will learn:** the settings that apply and save instantly.

For everyday changes you do not need to edit `config.json`: `:settings`, or `c` from the home screen, opens a menu with the eight settings people change most. Each row shows its current value and, when you choose another, it applies on the spot and is written to `config.json` without a restart.

The rows are Accordion folders, Explorer hide width, Icons & bullets, Dialog border, Transparent background, Heading numbers, Link suggestions and Language. The transparency row comes back in E06; the rest are practised here and in the next lessons.

Language picks the language of the interface and of the tutorial: Auto (system) follows your system's language, and English or Español fixes one. The change shows at once, without a restart.

Explorer hide width is the terminal width, in columns, below which the explorer hides to make room for the editor. It accepts 1 to 1000; the factory value is 120. If you lower it a lot, the explorer almost never hides; if you raise it, it hides sooner.

## Your turn

1. Run `:settings` (or press `c` on the home screen) and change any row.
2. Change the width at which the explorer hides, between 1 and 1000, and watch how it responds when you narrow the window.
