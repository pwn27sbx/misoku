# E01 · The shortcut map

**You will learn:** to discover the keys without memorizing them.

Misoku has dozens of shortcuts and you do not need to learn them by heart: the app shows them to you. `space` is the leader, just like in LazyVim. If you press it alone and wait, a guide appears with three columns: NOTE, VIEW and GO. Each column groups the keys by what they do, and the ones that open a submenu are marked with `+`.

`space o` opens the second page, the one with the "misoku" keys: the ones that start with `space o`. It is the page you will use most, because that is where checkboxes, links and the shortcuts for working with notes live.

The command palette, with `?` or `space h`, does the same in text: it lists each action next to the keys it has right now. And here is the important detail: it reads your `config.json`, not the factory defaults. As soon as you change a shortcut in the next lesson, the palette and the guide will show it.

## Your turn

1. Press `space` and wait without touching anything: the guide appears with NOTE, VIEW and GO.
2. Press `space o` and look at the second page with the misoku keys.
3. Press `?` and compare the keys in the palette with the ones in the guide.
