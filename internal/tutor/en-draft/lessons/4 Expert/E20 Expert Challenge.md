# E20 · Expert Challenge

**You will learn:** to make Misoku your own in five minutes, without a guide.

This challenge teaches nothing new: it brings the route together in a single working session. The idea is that you do not look at the earlier lessons unless you get stuck, and that the result is a configuration you want to keep.

Start with shortcuts and the theme, which is what you notice most. Then sync, which is what you appreciate most when you change machines. And finish with the guide and the palette, which are the ones that will get you out of trouble for the rest of the year.

## Your turn

1. Reassign a key and use it.
2. Change the theme and keep it.
3. Run a sync and read `:sync status`.
4. Open the guide with `space` and run something from the palette.

Leave a line down below with your favourite configuration: shortcut, theme and how you sync. That way you close the route with something written by you.
