# A18 · Moving blocks

**You will learn:** to reorder the note from the preview, by dragging, and to add a block exactly where you want it.

When the mouse passes over the preview, each block shows two handles in the left margin: `+` and a row of dots. The `+` opens a new line at that point with the `/` menu on it; the dotted handle grabs the whole block to move it.

Dragging does not copy or cut: it moves the block and closes the gaps, without leaving extra blank lines or gluing together sections that do not belong together. When you drop it, the text is reordered in the note, and `u` undoes it like any other edit.

It is the way to tidy a long note without selecting paragraphs by hand: you think in blocks, not in lines.
