# A07 · Mentions and backlinks

**You will learn:** what a note receives from the others: who links to it and who names it without linking.

Below the preview, Misoku lists the notes that link to this one. It is the half you do not see when you write a link: the link leaves from here and arrives there, and the list shows you the way back with the exact line where it is.

There is a second list, the unlinked mentions: notes that write this note's title without brackets. They help you discover connections you have not made yet. A click on a row opens that note, and the `[ link ]` button turns all its mentions into a real link in a single gesture.

Clicking a backlinks row opens the linking note and leaves the cursor on the link's line, not at the start. That way you follow a conversation between notes without searching by hand.
