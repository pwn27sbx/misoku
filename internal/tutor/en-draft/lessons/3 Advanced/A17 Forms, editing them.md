# A17 · Forms: editing them

**You will learn:** to change the form from the form itself, without editing the block by hand.

Each question has a menu when you click it: rename it, change its type, make it required, clear its answer or delete it entirely. The change is written into the block like any other edit, so it is saved and undone like the rest of the note.

At the bottom there is a button to add a question: you give it a name, choose the type and, if it has options, write them. The title and the description are also edited with a click: they are the block's two metadata lines.

Editing this way is safer than touching the syntax: the menu keeps the type and the options well written, and there is no way to leave a question half done.
