# A21 · Advanced Challenge

**You will learn:** nothing new. You are going to bring everything from this route together in a single project note, in the order you would really use it.

This challenge does not check separate steps: it asks you for a note that could exist in your vault tomorrow. Treat it as a rehearsal, calmly, and go back to whatever lesson you need if something does not work.

The note is called `House project` and lives on its data: properties, a status and a cover. Around it, the links do the work: an embed brings in the budget, a rename keeps it connected, a mention becomes a link and the graph shows that everything is joined. At the end, a PDF to send it.

When you finish, you will have the whole vault in your head: from the first heading to a form, from a link to a PDF.

## Your turn

1. Create `House project` with the properties `trip` and `stage`, `status: active` and a cover.
2. Create `Budget` with a line marked `^total` and embed that block in `House project`.
3. Rename `Budget` to `Accounts` and accept updating the links.
4. Export `House project` to PDF and open its local graph.
5. Review the note's unlinked mentions and turn one into a link.
