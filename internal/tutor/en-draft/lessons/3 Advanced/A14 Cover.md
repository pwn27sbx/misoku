# A14 · Cover

**You will learn:** to put a header image on a note and to see it as a band in the preview.

The `/` menu has a `Cover` item: you pick an image and Misoku saves it in the vault and writes its path in the front matter's `cover` property. It is not a link in the text: it is a property of the note, so it travels with it and can be changed without touching the body.

The preview draws it as a band of eight rows, above the title, cropped to the note's width. It is what makes a trip note or a recipe recognisable at a glance in the list.

The same image does not appear in the properties table: `cover` is presentation, and the table shows data. If you want to change the cover, go through `/Cover` again or edit that line by hand.

## Your turn

1. Press `/`, choose `Cover` and pick an image; if you do not have one at hand, write `cover: cover.png` in the front matter.
2. Look at the eight-row band with the image in the preview.
