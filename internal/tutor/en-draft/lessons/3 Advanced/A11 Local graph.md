# A11 · Local graph

**You will learn:** to see a note's links as a map and to walk it without touching the mouse.

`space g`, or `:graph`, opens a tab with the current note in the centre and its neighbours around it. It is the view of a single note: what it links to and what links to it, drawn with arrows by direction.

Inside the graph, the keys are the usual ones: `h` and `l` go to the parent and the child, `j` and `k` to the siblings, `Enter` opens the node, `+` and `-` zoom in and out, `0` centres again and `Esc` takes you back to the note.

The mouse works too: a click selects a node, the wheel zooms, dragging moves the drawing and the buttons do the same as the keys. And it is not a snapshot: if you write a new link in another tab, the graph updates by itself.

## Your turn

1. Press `space g` (or run `:graph`) and see this note in the centre.
2. Move with `h`/`l` and `j`/`k`, open with `Enter`, zoom with `+`/`-`, centre with `0` and go back with `Esc`.
3. Click a node, use the wheel to zoom in, drag the drawing and try the buttons.
4. In another tab write a new link to this note and watch the graph update.
