# A16 · Forms: choices and stars

**You will learn:** the three ways to answer without typing: one option, several options and a star rating.

A single-choice question is written with the `(choice)` type and its options separated by bars: `? Where do you sleep? (choice): Cabin | Hotel | Apartment`. In the preview it is answered with a click; another click on the same option clears the answer, so you can change your mind without deleting anything by hand.

Multiple choice is the same with the `(multi)` type. Each click adds or removes an option, and the answer keeps them joined by commas in the order they are written, not the order you clicked them.

The rating is the `(rating)` type: five stars and an answer with the number. The third star writes `3`. All three write the answer on the line below, so the result reads in the text and can be fixed by hand.
