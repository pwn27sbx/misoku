# I28 · The self-folding explorer

**You will learn:** to let the tree tidy itself and to see changes made outside.

The explorer is an accordion: when you open a folder, its sibling on the same level closes, so the tree doesn't grow out of control. The Accordion folders row in `:settings` turns it on and off at once, and it is saved as `ui.accordion`. The tree also watches the folder: if you create or delete a file from outside Misoku, within a couple of seconds it appears or disappears without restarting.

Have two folders with notes in view in the explorer.
