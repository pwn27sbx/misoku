# I20 · Links to headings

**You will learn:** to link a specific part of a note.

A normal link leads to a whole note. With a hash after the name you lead to a heading inside it: `[[#Packing]]` is the heading `Packing` in this note and `[[Trip#Packing]]` the one in another note. The hash is the separator because the note goes on the left and what you are looking for inside it on the right, and it is the same sign that starts a heading: it reads as "this note, the heading Packing". When you type `[[` the list shows you the headings of the open note; with `Trip#` in front, those of that note.

Prepare the note `Trip` with a heading `Packing`, another note called `Plan`, and a level-two heading, `## Packing`, in this note as well.

## Your turn

1. Type `[[#` and see that the list brings only the headings of this note.
2. Pick `Packing`: you get `[[#Packing]]`; put the cursor on the link and press `gd`.
3. Type `[[Trip#` and look at the headings of `Trip`.
4. Complete `[[Trip#Packing]]` here and write the same link inside `Plan`.
5. In `:settings`, set Link suggestions to Finder, open `[[` and look at the footer of the list.
