# I24 · Tags

**You will learn:** the five ways to tag a note.

A tag is a word with a hash in front: `#travel`. A slash makes it finer: `#travel/beach` is a tag inside another and both count. Properties can hold tags too, and there YAML accepts three forms: separated by commas, in square brackets, or as a list with one dash per line. Tags in the body classify the text; tags in the properties classify the whole note.

For this lesson, add a properties block at the top of the note: two lines of three dashes and, between them, your keys.

## Your turn

1. Write `#travel` in the body.
2. Write `#travel/beach`.
3. Inside the properties, write `tags: beach, sun`.
4. Change it to `tags: [beach, sun]`.
5. Change it to a list: one line with `- beach` and another with `- sun`.
