# I16 · Charts

**You will learn:** to turn numbers into bars.

A chart is a `chart` fence with `type: bar` and one `Label: number` line per value. The preview draws it with bars; the `+ Add value` button adds a value, and clicking a bar edits the value or the label, or deletes it. With two lines of numbers you already have a chart you can read at a glance.

## Your turn

1. Insert `/Chart`.
2. Click `+ Add value` and add `Beach: 3`.
3. Click a bar, rename it to `Museum: 4` and finally delete it: the step is recorded when you change it.
