# I11 · Columns

**You will learn:** to split content into two, three and four columns.

A columns block is a `~~~columns` fence with a `+++` separator between one column and the next; the `/` menu writes the complete block and you only fill it in. When the window is narrow the columns stack one under the other, so they read well on any screen.

## Your turn

1. Insert `/2 columns` and write a sentence in each column.
2. Insert `/3 columns`.
3. Insert `/4 columns`.
4. Look at the columns in the preview and make the window narrower: they stack.
