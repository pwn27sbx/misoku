# I22 · Aliases and the suggestion mode

**You will learn:** to show a text different from the target and to choose how links are suggested.

A bar inside the brackets separates the target from the visible text: `[[Trip|my trip]]` leads to `Trip` but reads "my trip". Links are suggested in two ways: Inline, a dropdown under the cursor while the text stays in the note, and Finder, the finder with a footer that reminds you of the keys. The Link suggestions row in `:settings` switches from one to the other at once, without restarting, and the mode you choose is saved in the configuration.

Prepare the note `Trip` and a note with the tag `#travel`.

## Your turn

1. Write `[[Trip|my trip]]`.
2. In `:settings`, change Link suggestions to Finder and back to Inline.
3. In Finder, type `[[tag:travel` and pick a note; in Inline, `tag:` is plain text.
