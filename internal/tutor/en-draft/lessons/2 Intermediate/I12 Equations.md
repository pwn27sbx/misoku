# I12 · Equations

**You will learn:** to write maths that draws itself.

An equation is written in LaTeX inside a `math` block, and the preview draws it with symbols, with nothing to install. The two-line `$$` shortcut works too: everything between them is drawn the same way. The `/` menu writes the fence and you only add the formula.

## Your turn

1. Insert `/Equation` and write `a^2 + b^2 = c^2` inside the block.
2. Write by hand a block with two `$$` lines and, between them, `e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0`.
3. Look at the two equations drawn in the preview.
