# I00 · Intermediate

The Intermediate route assumes you know the Basic route: writing, saving, the explorer, tabs, headings, lists, checkboxes, links and views. Here you learn to move and edit with the speed of Vim, and the blocks that turn a note into a working notebook: tables, columns, equations, boards, calendars, timelines, charts, images and links that hit their target.

Each lesson creates its own notes, so none depends on an earlier file and you can do them in any order. When a step is not marked as done, the status bar tells you what is missing: read the task again and try once more.
