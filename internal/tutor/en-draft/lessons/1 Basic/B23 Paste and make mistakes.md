# B23 · Paste and make mistakes

**You will learn:** to paste with `ctrl+v` and to read the errors in the bar.

In INSERT, `ctrl+v` pastes the text from the clipboard; in NORMAL, `p` pastes after the cursor. When something goes wrong, the status bar turns red with the message, and `Esc` closes it. Errors don't break anything: they are Misoku's way of telling you what it expected. If the clipboard is empty, copy a line with `yy` and paste it with `p`.

## Your turn

1. Copy some text from another window and paste it with `ctrl+v` on a new line.
2. Cause an error with `:graph foo`, look at the red bar and close it with `Esc`.
