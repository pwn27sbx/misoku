# B18 · Quotes and divider

**You will learn:** a quote with `> ` and a horizontal line with `---`.

A quote starts with `>` and a space; in the preview it is drawn with a bar on the left. A line with three hyphens separates sections. Both are one-line blocks: the `/` menu writes them and you only add the text. The divider goes on its own line, with no spaces or text around it.

## Your turn

1. Write a quote with `/Quote` (or `> `).
2. Separate two sections with `/Divider` (or `---`).
