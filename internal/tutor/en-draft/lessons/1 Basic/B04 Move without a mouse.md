# B04 · Move without a mouse

**You will learn:** to reach any part of the note with `h`, `j`, `k`, `l`, the arrows, `0`, `^`, `$`, `gg` and `G`.

`h` goes left, `j` down, `k` up and `l` right; the arrows do the same and you can mix them with the letters. `0` and `^` take you to the start of the line, `$` to the end, and `Home` and `End` do the same. For the whole note, `gg` goes up to the start and `G` down to the end.

## Your turn

1. Move around the list with `h`, `j`, `k` and `l` until you reach the corners.
2. Do the same walk with the arrows.
3. Jump to the start and the end of a line with `0`, `^`, `Home`, `$` and `End`.
4. Jump to the start and the end of the note with `gg` and `G`.
