# B16 · Checkboxes: remove and click

**You will learn:** to remove a checkbox with `space o C` and to check off tasks with the mouse from the preview.

`space o C` removes the box and leaves the item as text: `- [x] task` becomes `- task`. In the split preview, each task draws its box; a click on the box (only on the box, not on the text) checks it off, and another click unchecks it. That way you can go through a list with the mouse without touching the Markdown.

## Your turn

1. On the checked task, press `space o C`: the checkbox disappears and you are left with `- Book the museum`.
2. In the preview, click the box of `Water the plants` and then once more to uncheck it.
