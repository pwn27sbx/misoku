# B10 · Rename and delete

**You will learn:** to rename with `r` and to delete with `d`, which sends the note to the Trash.

Renaming doesn't break links: Misoku rewrites the ones that pointed to the note. Deleting isn't final either: `d` asks for confirmation and moves the file to the system Trash, so you can get it back from your desktop.

## Your turn

1. With the explorer focused, select `groceries` and press `r`; type `market` and press `enter`.
2. Select `market` and press `d`; confirm the deletion.
