# B09 · Open and create notes

**You will learn:** to move through the explorer's tree, open a note with `enter`, and create a note and a folder with `n`.

The explorer is the list of folders and notes in the vault. With the focus on it, `j` and `k` go down and up, `enter` opens the selected note or expands a folder, and `n` asks for a name: `groceries` creates a note and `ideas/`, with a slash at the end, creates a folder.

## Your turn

1. With the explorer focused (`space e`), walk the tree with `j` and `k` and open a note with `enter`.
2. Create a note with `n` and the name `groceries`.
3. Create a folder with `n` and the name `ideas/`.

## Your practice area

The explorer is on the left: `space e` gives it the focus, and `space e` or `Esc` take you back to the editor.
