# B08 · The explorer and focus

**You will learn:** `space e` as in LazyVim, the three areas of the screen and how to move the focus.

The screen has three areas: the explorer on the left, the editor in the middle and the preview on the right. Only one has the focus, and the letters you type go to that one. `space e` opens the explorer and gives it the focus; if it already has it, it closes it and takes you back to the editor. `ctrl+b` only shows or hides it without moving the focus, and you don't need to learn it.

In a narrow window the explorer covers the editor; `Esc` closes it. `tab` and `shift+tab` go through the areas in order, `ctrl+h` and `ctrl+l` go left and right, and you can drag the divider between editor and preview with the mouse.

## Your turn

1. Press `space e`: the explorer opens and takes the focus. Press it again: it closes and the editor comes back.
2. Make the window narrower until the explorer covers the editor, and close it with `Esc`.
3. Look at the three areas: explorer, editor and preview.
4. Drag the divider between editor and preview.
5. Move the focus with `tab`, `shift+tab`, `ctrl+h` and `ctrl+l`.
