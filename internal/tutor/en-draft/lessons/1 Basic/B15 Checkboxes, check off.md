# B15 · Checkboxes: check off

**You will learn:** the three ways to check off a task: typing the checkbox, `space o c` and `Enter` in NORMAL, plus checking off several with `V`.

A task is `- [ ] text`; checking it off changes the checkbox to `- [x] `. `space o c` toggles the checkbox on the line or on the whole selection, `space o C` removes it, and in NORMAL `Enter` on a task toggles it too. With `V` you select whole lines and `space o c` checks them all off at once.

## Your turn

1. In the first task, type `[x] ` before the text to check it off.
2. In the second task, put the cursor on it and press `space o c`.
3. Select the next three tasks with `V` and press `space o c` once.
4. In NORMAL, put the cursor on the last task and press `Enter`.
