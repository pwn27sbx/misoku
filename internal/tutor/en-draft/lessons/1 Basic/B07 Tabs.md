# B07 · Tabs

**You will learn:** to switch notes without the mouse with `L` and `H`, `]` and `[`, `space tab` and `space shift+tab`, and the `+` in the bar.

Each open note is a tab in the top row. `L` goes to the next one and `H` to the previous one, as in LazyVim; `]` and `[` do the same with a single key, and `space tab` and `space shift+tab` are the long forms. If the focus is on the preview of a note with a calendar, `H` and `L` move the calendar's month instead of the tab: focus decides.

## Your turn

1. With two notes open, go to the next one with `L` and come back with `H`.
2. Also try `]`, `[`, `space tab` and `space shift+tab`.
3. Click a tab, close it with `space x` and create a new one with the `+` on the right.

## Your practice area

If you only have one tab, open another one here with `space n` and save something with `ctrl+s`.
