# B21 · Reading view and editor view

**You will learn:** the reading view with `space p`, the editor view with `space r`, and how to get back to the split.

`space p` shows the note alone at full width, drawn like the preview: the markers hide and headings, lists, quotes and tables look like a document. And you can still edit: in normal mode everything stays drawn, including the block under the cursor, which a stripe marks, and `j` and `k` jump from block to block. When you press `i` that block shows its Markdown so you can type, and `Esc` draws it again; you delete and undo just as in the editor. `space r` shows only the editor, with line numbers and all the Markdown in view. Pressing the same key again brings back the usual split.

## Your turn

1. Press `space p`: reading view, full width and drawn like the preview.
2. Press `space r`: only the Markdown, with line numbers.
3. Press `space r` again: the split comes back.
4. Press `space p` again and watch the markers hide, in the cursor's block too; `i` shows its Markdown and `Esc` draws it again.
5. Look at the note in reading view: headings, lists, quotes and tables, all drawn.
