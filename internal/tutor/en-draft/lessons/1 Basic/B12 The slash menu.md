# B12 · The / menu

**You will learn:** the menu that writes Markdown for you: open it with `/`, filter, confirm with `Enter` and close it with `Esc`.

In INSERT, at the start of a line or after a space, `/` opens a menu with every block: headings, lists, quotes, tables, calendars and more. You type to filter by the block's name, `Enter` inserts its Markdown and `Esc` closes the menu without touching the note. It is the way to write Markdown without remembering the syntax. The menu also opens when you type `/` right after a space, and the arrows choose the block.

## Your turn

1. On an empty line, in INSERT, press `/`: the menu opens.
2. Filter by typing `quote` and press `Enter`: a quote appears.
3. Open the menu again and close it with `Esc` without inserting anything.
