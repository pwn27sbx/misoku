# B25 · Quit and come back

**You will learn:** to close Misoku without losing anything: `:q` asks first, `:wq` saves and quits, `:q!` quits without saving.

`ctrl+c` closes the app, but the command line gives you finer control. `:q` closes and, if there are unsaved changes, asks first with three buttons: `Save All` saves them, `Discard` throws them away and `Cancel` lets you carry on. `:wq` (or `:x`) saves and quits. `:q!` quits without saving. When you run `misoku tutor` again you resume at the first step you haven't passed, so quitting doesn't lose your progress.

## Your turn

1. With unsaved changes, press `:q`: the app asks before quitting. Choose `Cancel` to stay.
2. Press `:wq` (or `:x`): it saves and quits.
3. Open Misoku again and quit with `:q!` without saving.
