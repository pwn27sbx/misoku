# B11 · Fold and peek

**You will learn:** to fold folders with `l`, `h`, `enter` and `z`, to close them all with `W`, and to preview without leaving with `p`.

In the tree, `l` opens the selected folder and `h` closes it or takes you to its parent folder. `enter` on a folder opens or closes it without opening any note, and `z` folds or unfolds it without moving you. `W` closes every folder at once. `p` shows the selected note in the preview without leaving the explorer: ideal for skimming a folder.

## Your turn

1. Fold and unfold a folder with `l`, `h`, `enter` and `z`.
2. Close every folder with `W`.
3. With the explorer focused, preview a note with `p`.
