# I14 · Calendars

**You will learn:** to keep events in a calendar that writes into the note itself.

The block keeps the month on the `month:` line and each event is a line with the date and the text. In the preview, clicking a day opens an event; clicking an event renames it, moves it to another day or deletes it; the `Today` button in the header goes back to the current month and the arrows on either side change it. With the preview focused, `<` and `>` move the month and `T` goes back to today.

## Your turn

1. Insert `/Calendar`.
2. Click a day and write `Beach` as the event.
3. Click the event, rename it to `Museum`, move it to another day and finally delete it: the step is recorded when you rename it.
4. Click `Today`, between the arrows of the calendar header.
5. With the preview focused, use `<` and `>` and press `T`.
