# I03 · Copying lines and pasting

**You will learn:** to duplicate and move whole lines with two letters.

Visual selection is for a piece of text; for a whole line there is something shorter: `yy` copies it and `p` pastes it below. Since the cursor moves down to the copy, `yy` and several `p` repeat the line as many times as you like, and with `dd` and `p` you move it somewhere else without retyping it.

## Your turn

1. Write the line `beach list`, put the cursor on it and press `yy`.
2. Press `p`: you will see two identical lines in a row.
