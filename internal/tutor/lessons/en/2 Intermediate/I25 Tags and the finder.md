# I25 · Tags and the finder

**You will learn:** to find notes by tag and by text.

A tag is not much use if you cannot search for it. `:tags` opens the list of the vault's tags and takes you to the notes that carry one. In the finder (`ctrl+p`), `tag:travel` filters by tag and `#travel` does the same; in the `[[` finder the hash means headings, not tags. The finder looks at the name and the content, and the notes in `Plantillas/`, the templates folder, stay out of the tag list: they are moulds, not notes.

Prepare a note with `#travel` and the folder `Plantillas` with the note `daily` inside.

## Your turn

1. Run `:tags` and pick a tag from the list.
2. In the finder (`ctrl+p`), type `tag:travel`.
3. Clear it and type `#travel`: in this finder the hash means a tag.
4. Search by name and by text with `ctrl+p`.
5. Open `:tags` again and check that the note in `Plantillas/` doesn't show up.
