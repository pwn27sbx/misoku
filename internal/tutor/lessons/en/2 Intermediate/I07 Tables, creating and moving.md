# I07 · Tables: creating and moving

**You will learn:** to insert a table and walk through its cells without the mouse.

A Markdown table is text: a row of cells, a row of dashes that marks where each column starts, and the rest of the rows. The `/` menu inserts one with two columns, separator row included, and `Tab` jumps to the next cell while you keep typing. Inside a table the status bar changes and reminds you of the cell keys, and the `/` menu itself opens with the row and column entries at the top, which is where the most used commands are.

## Your turn

1. Insert `/Table` on an empty line.
2. Fill in two cells, move from one to the other with `Tab`, and go back with `Shift+Tab`.
3. With the cursor inside the table, look at the hint in the status bar.
4. Press `/` inside the table: the menu opens with the table entries.
