# I05 · Collapsible blocks and callouts

**You will learn:** to hide a summary behind a fold and to write three kinds of callout.

A collapsible block is a bit of HTML: `<details>` opens the block and `<summary>` is the line you see when it is closed. The `/` menu writes the whole thing, so there is nothing to memorise. Callouts are quotes with a label in square brackets: `[!NOTE]`, `[!TIP]` and `[!WARNING]`. The label is not decoration: the preview recognises it and changes the callout's colour and icon, which is why `[!TIP]` and `[!WARNING]` are typed by hand where the menu doesn't reach.

## Your turn

1. Insert `/Toggle list` and write a summary inside.
2. Insert `/Callout`.
3. Write two callouts by hand: one with `> [!TIP]` and another with `> [!WARNING]`.

Each callout is a quote: `>` and a space start the line, and the text goes below.
