# I15 · Timelines

**You will learn:** to draw dates on a line.

A span is a line `Name: date -> date`, inside a `timeline` fence. The preview draws each span as a bar between its two dates: `+ New` creates one, and clicking the span moves it, stretches it, shortens it, renames it or deletes it. Dates are written as year-month-day, the order nobody can misread.

## Your turn

1. Insert `/Timeline`.
2. Click `+ New` and create a span called `Trip`.
3. Click the span, rename it to `Coast`, stretch it and finally delete it: the step is recorded when you rename it.
