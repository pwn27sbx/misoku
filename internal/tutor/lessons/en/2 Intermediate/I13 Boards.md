# I13 · Boards

**You will learn:** to move cards between columns with the mouse.

A board is a `board` fence with columns that start with `## ` and cards that start with `- `. The preview draws it as a kanban: each column with its `+ New page` button at the bottom and each card with its menu. Clicking a card lets you rename it, move it to another column or delete it; clicking the header lets you add a card, rename the column or delete it along with its cards.

## Your turn

1. Insert `/Board`.
2. Click `+ New page` in a column and write the card `Backpack`.
3. Click the card `Backpack`, rename it to `Boots`, move it to another column and finally delete it: the step is recorded as soon as you rename it.
4. Click the header of a column and rename it to `Finished`.
