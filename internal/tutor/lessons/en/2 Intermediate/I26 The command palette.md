# I26 · The command palette

**You will learn:** to find any command without remembering its key.

The palette is the list of everything the app can do, with its key beside it. `?` (or `space h`) opens it and you type to filter: `table` takes you to the table commands and `settings` to the configuration; `Enter` runs it. It is also where you see the keys you configured yourself, and where the EXPORT entries come first because they are the most searched for. `:messages` keeps what the bar said and you missed.

## Your turn

1. Press `?` (or `space h`) and filter by `table`.
2. Run `:settings` from the palette.
3. In the finder, open a result in another tab with `ctrl+t`.
4. Open `:messages` and read the latest messages with their time.
5. See that the EXPORT entries come first in the palette.
