# I01 · Words, pages and fine scrolling

**You will learn:** to travel through a long note with three jumps: a word, half a screen and a whole screen.

`hjkl` moves the cursor one character at a time, and for a short note that is plenty. When the note grows there are better shortcuts, and they are the same as Vim's: `w` and `b` jump by words; `ctrl+d` and `ctrl+u` go down and up half a screen; `pgdn`, `ctrl+f` and `pgup` move forward and back a whole screen. All of this happens in NORMAL, with letters as commands and the cursor staying wherever you leave it.
