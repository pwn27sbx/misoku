# I29 · Moving around the preview

**You will learn:** to read a long note without touching the editor.

The preview has its own focus and its own keys: `tab` takes you there and it lights up. There `j`/`k` move line by line, `d`/`u` half a screen, `space` a screen, `pgdn` another one, `g`/`G` to the top and the bottom, and the mouse wheel does the same. Headings fold with their triangle, `zM` closes them all and `zR` opens them. It is the way to go over a long note with no risk of typing over it.
