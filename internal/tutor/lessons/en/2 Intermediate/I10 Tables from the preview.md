# I10 · Tables from the preview

**You will learn:** to edit the table with the mouse, from the preview.

The preview is not only for looking. Under each table in the preview there are buttons to add a row or a column, clicking a cell opens it for typing, and clicking the header renames the column. Everything you do there ends up in the note's text, saved the usual way.

## Your turn

1. Insert `/Table`, keep the preview in view and click an empty cell: type `Beach`.
2. Click `+ Add row` and `+ Add column`, under the table.
3. Click the header of the second column and rename it to `Cost`.
