# A00 · Advanced

**You will learn:** the features that turn the vault into a real archive: properties, statuses, filters, history, mentions, embeds, graphs, export and forms.

This route assumes you know Basic and Intermediate: links, tables, tags, views and the finder. They are not repeated, and each lesson practises something that only exists here.

There are twenty-one short lessons, two to four minutes each. You can take them in order or jump straight to the one you care about, and each route's progress is saved separately.

When you finish, the Advanced Challenge asks you to build a complete project note with everything you learned.
