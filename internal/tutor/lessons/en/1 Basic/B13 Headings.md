# B13 · Headings

**You will learn:** the four heading levels the menu offers: from `/Heading 1` to `/Heading 4`.

A heading starts with `#` and a space; the more `#`, the smaller the level. In the editor you see the `#`, and in the preview and the reading view they are drawn as real headings: only their text, in bold and in the colour of their level. In the reading view, the heading under the cursor shows its `#` again so you can edit it. Headings are also link targets: `[[Note#Heading]]` jumps to them. Each heading goes on its own line, and the menu leaves the cursor after the `#` so you can type the text.
