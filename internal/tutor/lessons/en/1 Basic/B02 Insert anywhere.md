# B02 · Insert anywhere

**You will learn:** the five doors into INSERT besides `i`: `a`, `A`, `I`, `o` and `O`.

`i` inserts before the cursor and `a` after it; the difference matters when the cursor sits on a letter. `I` and `A` jump to the ends of the line without walking it, and `o` and `O` open a new line below or above without fighting with `Enter`. With these five doors you can type anywhere in a note without touching the mouse.

## Your turn

1. On the line `cat`, put the cursor on the `t` and press `a`; type `s`.
2. Press `A` and type ` · suitcase` (space, middle dot, space, suitcase).
3. Press `I` and type `01 · ` (zero one, space, middle dot, space).
4. Press `o` and type `packing list` on the new line below.
5. Press `O` and type `— flight —` on the new line above.
