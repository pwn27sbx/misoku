# B24 · The Misoku home screen

**You will learn:** the home screen: recent notes, folders and menus, with their fixed letters.

`space d` goes back to the home screen without closing your tabs. At the top are the recent notes and the folders; at the bottom, the menus with their letter. The numbers `1` to `9` open the first recent note, the second… and then the first folder, the second…; the letters `n`, `f`, `e`, `t`, `?`, `g`, `c`, `s`, `l` and `q` are fixed shortcuts, and `Enter` runs the selected entry.

## Your turn

1. Press `space d` and look at the home screen: recent notes, projects and menus.
2. Press `1` to open the first recent note; come back with `space d` and press `2` for the first folder.
3. Try the fixed letters `n`, `f`, `e`, `t`, `?`, `g`, `c`, `s`, `l`, `q` and `Enter`, coming back with `space d` after each one.

`q` closes Misoku: leave it for last and, if you quit by accident, run `misoku tutor` again and you will be right back here.
