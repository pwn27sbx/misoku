# E08 · The theme picker

**You will learn:** to try themes without fear and to keep the one you like.

`space t`, or `:colo` without a name, opens the theme picker. As you move through the list, the theme applies right away: you can see how the whole app looks before deciding. `Enter` keeps it and saves it in `config.json`; `Esc` cancels and brings back the theme you had, leaving no trace in the file.

Misoku includes 97 NvChad palettes, sorted by name, from `aquarium` to `zenburn`. You move through them with `j` and `k` or the arrow keys; the list is long, so it is fine to scroll down fast.

The order matters: first you look, then you decide. If you jump from theme to theme without pressing `Enter`, everything is back as it was when you leave with `Esc`.

## Your turn
