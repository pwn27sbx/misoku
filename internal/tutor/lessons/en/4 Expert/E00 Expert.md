# Expert route

**You will learn:** to make Misoku your own: shortcuts, look, folders and sync.

The earlier routes taught you to write and to get around. This one teaches the other half of Misoku: the `config.json` file, your own shortcuts, themes, the vault's folders, sync with git and the global graph. No lesson depends on a note created earlier, but everything from Basic, Intermediate and Advanced is taken as known.

`config.json` lives outside the vault: on Linux in `~/.config/misoku/config.json`, on macOS in `~/Library/Application Support/misoku/config.json` and on Windows in `%APPDATA%\misoku\config.json`. That is why it never mixes with your notes, and why sync never touches it.

Every change is read at startup; if something does not respond, save the file and open Misoku again.
